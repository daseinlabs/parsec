03

type & usage

Typeface: JetBrains Mono (free, OFL) — display, UI, and code.  ·  Clear space: keep ≥ one sightline gap around the mark.  ·  On light: use the emerald #17833A for UI and #2FA317 for the logo — never neon phosphor on white.  ·  Don't rotate, stretch, recolor per-campaign, or fill the mark.  ·  Voice: terse, benefit-first — lead with the number.
